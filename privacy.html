Sudoku Daily Breeze Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

The short version

Sudoku Daily Breeze (installed as "Sudoku Breeze") does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your best times, settings and the puzzle in progress are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Variety Pack is sold as a one-time in-app purchase through Apple's App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment and the receipt; we never see your payment details. A purchase can be restored on another device signed in to the same store account with Restore Purchases in the app.

Network use

On launch the app downloads a small configuration file that lets us switch the pack off temporarily if a problem is found. This request contains no information about you or your device beyond what any web request carries, and nothing is logged.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
